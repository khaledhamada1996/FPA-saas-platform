# SaaS Business Model

## Positioning

The product is a B2B SaaS FP&A platform. It should be positioned as a planning, forecasting, analysis, and decision-support layer rather than an accounting replacement.

## Packaging Principle

Use progressive tiers based on business complexity and value, not arbitrary feature fragmentation.

Potential structure:

- Starter: small company, limited users/dimensions, core planning and dashboards.
- Growth: more users, departments, workflows, scenarios, and reporting.
- Business: advanced planning, integrations, controls, and analytics.
- Enterprise: consolidation, SSO, advanced governance, higher limits, and enterprise support.

Pricing is intentionally not fixed in this document. It should be determined after validating target customer profile, willingness to pay, usage drivers, infrastructure costs, and competitive positioning.

## Likely Value Drivers

- Number of users.
- Number of entities/branches.
- Planning complexity.
- Data volume.
- Integrations.
- Advanced workflow/governance.
- AI usage.
- Consolidation requirements.

## Commercial Principle

The free/low-cost development environment is not automatically the production hosting strategy. Production infrastructure must be selected according to commercial terms, database requirements, security, reliability, and expected usage.
