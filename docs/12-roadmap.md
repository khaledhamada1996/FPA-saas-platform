# Product Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Product Definition

- Product vision.
- Requirements.
- Domain model.
- Security model.
- Architecture.
- MVP acceptance criteria.

## Phase 1 — MVP

- Auth and organizations.
- Users and RBAC.
- Company structure.
- Excel/CSV import.
- Mapping and validation.
- Financial model.
- Budget.
- Forecast.
- Actual vs Budget/Forecast.
- Basic cash forecast.
- KPI dashboard.
- Scenarios.
- Grounded AI analyst.
- Basic reports and audit log.

## Phase 2 — Workflow & Integrations

- Department submission workflows.
- Approval chains.
- Advanced mapping.
- ERP/accounting integrations.
- CRM/payroll/operational integrations.
- Advanced cash planning.
- Driver-based planning.
- Alerts.
- Action management.

## Phase 3 — Enterprise FP&A

- Multi-company groups.
- Consolidation.
- Advanced dimensional planning.
- Enterprise permissions.
- SSO.
- Advanced forecasting.
- Board-pack automation.
- Advanced scenario modeling.
- Enterprise governance and audit capabilities.

## Phase 4 — AI Financial Intelligence

- AI forecast builder.
- AI scenario assistant.
- Automated management commentary.
- Proactive risk detection.
- Decision/action recommendations.
- Natural-language planning workflows with controlled approvals.

Roadmap order may change after user validation, technical discovery, security review, and commercial evidence.
