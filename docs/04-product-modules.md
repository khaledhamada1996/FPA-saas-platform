# Product Modules

## 1. Workspace & Organization
Company profile, fiscal calendar, currency, entities, branches, departments, cost centers, products, projects, and configuration.

## 2. Identity & Access
Authentication, invitations, roles, permissions, scopes, sessions, and organization membership.

## 3. Data Import & Collection
Excel/CSV upload, structured templates, department submissions, validation, import history, and controlled adjustments.

## 4. Mapping Engine
Maps source accounts/categories and operational data into a standardized FP&A model. Mapping rules must be reusable and versioned.

## 5. Financial Model
Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, working capital, capex, debt, headcount, and unit economics.

## 6. Budgeting
Annual/monthly budgets, top-down and bottom-up planning, dimensional planning, assumptions, and approval versions.

## 7. Forecasting
Rolling forecasts, forecast versions, historical trends, seasonality, growth assumptions, and driver-based forecasts.

## 8. Scenario & What-If
Base/Best/Worst scenarios and controlled changes to assumptions such as sales, pricing, costs, hiring, capex, and branches.

## 9. Cash Planning
Opening cash, collections, payments, financing, capex, working capital, and projected closing cash.

## 10. Performance Analytics
Actual vs Budget, Actual vs Forecast, variance analysis, profitability, KPIs, and trends.

## 11. Executive Dashboard
Financial health, forecast outlook, cash, KPI performance, exceptions, and management attention items.

## 12. Reporting & Board Pack
Management reporting, recurring reports, executive summaries, and board-ready reporting.

## 13. Alerts & Actions
Threshold-based alerts and conversion of identified problems into assigned management actions.

## 14. AI Financial Analyst
Data-grounded financial Q&A, explanations, summaries, scenario reasoning, and recommendations.

## 15. Administration & Governance
Audit logs, version control, approvals, period/version locking, integrations, billing, and platform monitoring.

## Module Dependency Principle

Source data flows into the financial model before planning and analytics. Reports and AI consume governed model outputs rather than directly interpreting arbitrary raw uploads.
