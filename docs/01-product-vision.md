# Product Vision

## 1. Purpose

Build a SaaS platform specialized in Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) that helps companies understand where they are, where they are going, why performance is changing, and what management can do next.

The platform is a financial intelligence and planning layer above existing accounting, ERP, CRM, payroll, operational, and spreadsheet systems. It is not intended to replace an accounting system or ERP.

## 2. Problem

Companies often have financial and operational data distributed across multiple systems and spreadsheets. Finance teams then spend significant time collecting data, cleaning it, mapping accounts, preparing budgets, updating forecasts, explaining variances, and producing management reports.

The product addresses the gap between **data availability** and **management decision-making**.

## 3. Product Promise

The platform should make the FP&A cycle structured, repeatable, auditable, and understandable:

**Collect → Validate → Model → Budget → Forecast → Compare → Explain → Simulate → Decide → Act**

## 4. Target Customers

- Small companies that need structured budgeting, forecasting, cash planning, and management visibility.
- Medium companies with multiple departments, branches, cost centers, and finance stakeholders.
- Large organizations requiring scalable planning, permissions, integrations, consolidation, and executive reporting.

The architecture supports all three segments. Product complexity is progressively exposed rather than creating separate products.

## 5. Product Principles

1. Financial model first: analytics must be based on a governed financial model.
2. Decision-oriented: every important metric should help answer a management question.
3. Explainable: variances and AI answers must be traceable to underlying data and assumptions.
4. Auditable: important changes, submissions, approvals, and versions must be recorded.
5. Multi-tenant by design: tenant isolation is a foundational security requirement.
6. Progressive complexity: small companies should not be forced through enterprise workflows.
7. Integration-friendly: Excel/CSV first, APIs and business-system integrations later.
8. AI as an analyst: AI assists analysis and planning; it does not silently alter financial records.

## 6. Long-Term Vision

Become a decision-support layer for finance teams: a system where actual performance, budgets, forecasts, scenarios, cash, KPIs, operational drivers, risks, and management actions are connected in one financial planning model.
